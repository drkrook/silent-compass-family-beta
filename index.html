    🛣️ Offline roads & elevation (new)

    Download a town-sized road network and measured USGS elevation samples while online. Offline routes are calculated on your device using only downloaded roads. Roads are not checked for closures or passability.

    Sampled points are real measurements from the USGS elevation service, not a continuous DEM. Nearest-sample readings are labeled with distance and should not be treated as precise elevation at an arbitrary point.

    Enter start/destination in the Route guidance fields above. Exact coordinates and saved labels work offline; unknown addresses require an online lookup. Select a map resource marker as destination using the existing button.
